Terms & Conditions

Transparent rental guidelines, refundable security deposit rules, and cancellation policies designed for a safe and hassle-free journey.

Welcome to RideZone. These Terms & Conditions govern the rental and use of all motorized two-wheelers, electric scooters, and high-performance motorcycles made available through our platform. By reserving, booking, or operating any RideZone vehicle, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these terms in full.

01

Rental Eligibility & Verification

To ensure platform safety and statutory compliance under the Motor Vehicles Act, all riders must meet the following mandatory criteria prior to vehicle handover:

  • Minimum Age: The rider must be at least 18 years of age (or 21 years of age for high-end motorcycles including the KTM RC 390 and Kawasaki Ninja 300).
  • Valid Driving License: A permanent, valid Indian Driving License (or International Driving Permit along with a valid Passport) authorizing the operation of gear or non-gear two-wheelers. Learner's Licenses (LLR) are strictly not accepted.
  • Government Identity Proof: An original Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID must be presented for physical verification at pickup.
Strict Identity Verification The registered booking name must match the name on the driving license. Third-party driving or transferring the vehicle to unauthorized individuals is strictly prohibited and voids all coverage.
02

Bookings, Hourly Rates & Payments

RideZone operates on transparent hourly pricing with zero hidden surcharge fees. The rental charge begins at the scheduled pickup time selected during booking.

  • Transparent Pricing: All listed rates include standard maintenance, complimentary helmet, and comprehensive third-party insurance.
  • Fuel Policy: Vehicles are delivered with sufficient fuel to reach the nearest fueling station. Riders must return the vehicle with an equivalent fuel level. No cash refunds are provided for excess fuel upon return.
  • Payment Methods: We accept all standard digital payment instruments including UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, and Net Banking.
03

100% Refundable Security Deposit

To ensure the safe return and responsible usage of our fleet, a refundable security deposit is collected at the time of reservation:

  • Deposit Scale: Standard Scooters (Activa 6G, Access 125): ₹1,000 · Electric (Ather 450X): ₹1,500 · Street Bikes (MT-15, Pulsar NS200): ₹2,000 · Premium Supersports (KTM RC 390, Ninja 300): ₹3,000–₹3,500.
  • Refund Timeline: 100% of the deposit is automatically refunded to the original payment source immediately upon vehicle return and inspection verification.
  • Deductions: Deductions occur only in cases of verifiable accidental damage, unpaid traffic violations (challans), or extreme delayed return beyond agreed grace windows.
04

Cancellation & 1-Hour Full Refund Policy

We understand plans change. RideZone provides a risk-free cancellation guarantee:

1-Hour Full Refund Guarantee Any reservation cancelled within 1 hour of booking creation receives a 100% full refund with zero cancellation charges. Both the rental amount and security deposit are refunded immediately.

For cancellations requested after 1 hour from booking creation:

  • A nominal vehicle reservation fee (₹50–₹350 depending on category) is retained to compensate for blocked calendar availability.
  • The remaining rental balance and 100% of the security deposit are returned in full.
05

Responsible Vehicle Usage & Safety

Riders must operate the vehicle in strict accordance with traffic safety regulations and local laws:

  • Helmets Mandatory: Wearing an ISI-certified helmet is strictly mandatory for both the rider and pillion at all times.
  • Zero Tolerance: Riding under the influence of alcohol, narcotics, or intoxicating substances is strictly prohibited and subject to immediate vehicle confiscation and police reporting.
  • Speed & Stunts: Overspeeding beyond statutory limits, racing, stunting, or off-road misuse is prohibited.
06

Damage, Insurance & Liability

In the event of an unforeseen accident, collision, or mechanical issue during the rental period:

  • Immediate Reporting: Notify the RideZone support helpline immediately (+91 98765 43210). Do not attempt third-party unauthorized repairs.
  • Insurance Coverage: Standard third-party liability is covered under our master commercial fleet insurance policy.
  • Rider Liability: Rider liability for minor accidental cosmetic damage is capped at the security deposit amount, except in instances of gross negligence, reckless driving, or driving under the influence.
