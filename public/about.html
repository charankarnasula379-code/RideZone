ABOUT RIDEZONE

Freedom to Move.

An academic software engineering project created to deliver a luxury showroom experience, transparent hourly pricing, and effortless two-wheeler rentals.

Our Mission

To make two-wheeler urban and campus transit straightforward, transparent, and enjoyable through a verified, well-maintained fleet and instant digital reservation.

Zero Surcharges

What you see is what you pay. Rental rates are calculated transparently by the hour, with 100% refundable security deposits and clear cancellation policies.

Full-Stack Architecture

Engineered with an ACID-compliant MySQL database, real-time double-booking prevention, authenticated customer profiles, and a comprehensive administrative portal.

CURATED FLEET PHILOSOPHY

Quality Over Quantity

Rather than offering an unvetted marketplace, RideZone features an authoritative 7-vehicle fleet tailored for diverse riding needs:

  • ✓ Electric Mobility: High-performance smart electric scooter (Ather 450X)
  • ✓ Daily Commuter Scooters: Reliable city transit (Honda Activa 6G, Suzuki Access 125)
  • ✓ Naked Streetfighters: Responsive performance motorcycles (Yamaha MT-15 V2, Bajaj Pulsar NS200)
  • ✓ High-End Supersports: Track-inspired performance machines (KTM RC 390, Kawasaki Ninja 300)
